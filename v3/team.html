Who you will see at Alder Grove Dental

The people who look after reception, appointments and the day-to-day running of the practice.

Who you will be dealing with

  • The front of the practice

    Reception, the appointment book and the paperwork are looked after by the same people you meet when you come in.

  • Ask the right person first

    Appointments are made and changed by telephone or by email, and reception looks after the appointment book.

  • Before you come

    Access, parking and where exactly to come are questions for reception, ask when you book, by telephone or by email.
